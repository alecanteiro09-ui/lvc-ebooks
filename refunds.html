Refund Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Digital products

Our ebooks are digital content delivered immediately after payment. By completing your purchase you ask us to supply the content straight away and acknowledge that you lose your 14-day right to cancel once you have downloaded or accessed it.

When we will refund you

  • You were charged but did not receive your download link, and we cannot resolve it.
  • The file is faulty or corrupted and we cannot supply a working copy.
  • The ebook is materially different from its description.
  • You were charged more than once for the same order.

How to request a refund

Contact us through the contact page within 14 days of purchase, with your order email and a short description of the problem. We reply within 2 business days.

Timing

Approved refunds are returned to your original payment method. Your bank usually shows the money within 5 to 10 business days.
